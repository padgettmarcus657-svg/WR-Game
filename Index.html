The Vision

World Recognition is a creator-owned franchise built independently from the ground up — spanning gaming, film, television, animation, comics, merchandise, and live experiences. Every character, every world, and every conflict is part of a unified cosmology engineered for long-term expansion.

The Game

The flagship WR action RPG is the primary entry point into the universe. Built in the Godot Engine, the game blends fast combat, deep lore, and character-driven storytelling. Players uncover the truth behind the Recognition — a cosmic force that binds heroes, villains, and entire civilizations.

The Universe

Cosmic forces shape destinies. Factions rise and fall. The Recognition threads through every timeline, every character arc, and every world. WR is designed as a living universe — expanding through games, stories, and multimedia experiences.

The Creator

World Recognition is created by Marcus Brandon Padgett — narrative director, lead designer, and architect of the WR franchise. Every element is built with a commitment to ownership, clarity, and world-class transmedia strategy.
